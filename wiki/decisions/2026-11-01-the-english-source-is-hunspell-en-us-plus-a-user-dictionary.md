# The English source is hunspell en_US plus a user dictionary

**Ruling (2026-11-01, issue #160).** The maintainer's words, verbatim:

> "hunspell en_US 를 사용하고, 사용자 사전을 추가할 수 있게 하자."

## What it settles

- **`english_valid` is a dictionary lookup, not a word test over harvested
  text.** The shipped artefact is `libhunmin/data/modes/en-US.json`, generated by
  `tools/en-us2data.py` from hunspell `en_US` (`en_US.dic` + `en_US.aff`, SCOWL
  size 60) — 118,150 forms. Its provenance (`meta.json`, written by that
  generator on every run) records the inputs' SHA-256, the expansion method, the
  drop counts and what the list does not represent.
- **A person's own words are merged at lookup time.** `hunmin_buffered_preedit_
  load_user_words` / `..._add_user_word` take a plain file (one word per line,
  `#` comments); the engine never writes back to the shipped directory.
- **The Korean-typed Latin gap is accepted, and named.** `kbs` `mbc` `fifa`
  `uefa` `nhk` are in no English dictionary; the user dictionary is the ruled
  answer, and `WordSetTest.ShippedListAnswersForEnglishAndNotForTheKoreanTypedGap`
  asserts the gap so it cannot be quietly filled in later.

## What it does not settle

- **The curated supplement** priced beside the dictionaries was deferred, not
  adopted; nothing is built for it.
- **Separate distribution** of the word list (B3's mechanics) is its own
  follow-up: `hunmin-en-us` is a binary package with `Recommends` from the base
  package, and the APK and installer carry one dictionary in-tree. See
  [[2026-11-01-one-dict-by-default-the-release-page-later]].

## Why the record matters later

The measurement papers that priced four candidate dictionaries were deleted on
2026-11-08 with the rest of the retired modes measurement
([[2026-11-08-the-retired-corpus-measurement-is-deleted]]). What survives is the
ruling, the shipped artefact and its provenance — so a later session that wants
to revisit the choice re-derives the comparison with
`tools/modes-dict-probe.py`, rather than citing a figure that no longer has a
home. The requirement is `docs/SPEC-MODES.md` §ReqMode8; the file contract is
`docs/SPEC-SCHEMA.md` §English word list. See also
[[2026-11-01-b1-retired-the-hangul-judgement-needs-no-source]] for the Hangul
half of the same formula.
